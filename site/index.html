How it works

1. Submit

Your site's URL (up to 10 public pages you own or are authorized to test) and a short note on what visitors should be able to do.

2. We test

Every page is loaded on desktop (1366×768) and mobile (390×844). Links, assets, layout and load timing are checked.

3. You fix

Get a PDF + HTML report: a one-page owner summary with a Pass / Needs fixes / Fail verdict, then copy-ready developer tickets and full-page screenshots.

4. Retest

Fixed things? Request your one included retest and get an updated report to hand to your client.

What $149 includes — and what it doesn't

$149

One site, one-time. One retest included.

  • Up to 10 public pages, same domain
  • Full-page desktop + mobile screenshots of every page
  • Owner summary: Pass / Needs fixes / Fail verdict and top 5 issues in plain English
  • Developer tickets: title, severity, pages, steps to reproduce, expected vs actual, fix
  • Broken internal & external links (HTTP status); links that block automated checkers are listed as “check manually”, not broken
  • Mobile display problems (horizontal scrolling, missing viewport)
  • Missing content: broken images, failed scripts/styles/fonts
  • JavaScript console errors
  • Observable load issues with timings
  • Basics: page titles, meta descriptions, H1s, image alt text
  • Repeated issues merged into one ticket; highlighted screenshot evidence for every High-severity issue

Not included

  • Hosting, or making repairs for you
  • Unlimited support or calls
  • Security testing or certification of any kind
  • Accessibility/legal compliance certification
  • A promise that every issue is found — automated checks catch a lot, not everything
  • Pages behind logins, form submissions, payments or email delivery — these are clearly marked “not tested”

FAQ

Who is this for?

Small owner-led web agencies and freelancers who want an independent pre-launch check before handing a site to a client.

How long does it take?

The check itself is automated and takes minutes. Ordering isn’t open yet; delivery times will be confirmed at launch.

What’s the difference between the free preview and the $149 check?

The free preview fetches the raw HTML of one page and samples a few links — no browser, no screenshots. The paid check renders up to 10 pages in a real browser on desktop and mobile.

Will you change anything on my site?

No. We only load public pages like a visitor would. Nothing is submitted, purchased or modified.

What if a feature can't be tested?

It's marked “not tested” in the report so nobody assumes it was checked.

Can I talk to someone?

Proofday is deliberately no-call. Questions by email only (address coming soon).
